# AI-drafted advisories need an officer's approval before they leave the portal

An advisory joins hazard guidance with crop stage into advice for farmers, and readers may take it as NDMA's word. So the AI only drafts: an officer edits and approves each advisory, and only approved advisories can be exported or shared. Unapproved drafts are visible inside the portal and are marked as drafts.

## Current scope

External distribution is not planned. Officer sign-in, approval workflow, export and sharing are therefore deferred. The portal may generate and display clearly marked internal drafts, but it must not provide a way to publish, export or share them. This decision becomes an implementation requirement only if external distribution enters scope.
